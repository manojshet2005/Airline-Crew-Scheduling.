## Requirement

Provide three benefits and three challenges of using AI in the Agile work for Case 57, with evidence from lab results.

| Type | Claim | Case evidence | Limitation / control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benefit | AI can rapidly suggest boundary and invalid-input cases. | L1.5's ten AI-assisted cases were directly run; all ten matched expected behavior. | The test oracle came from sample rules and does not establish legal correctness. |
