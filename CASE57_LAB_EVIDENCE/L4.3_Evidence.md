## Structure and maintenance comparison

If the daily-hours field id changed from `daily` to `hours_today`:

| Version | Places that must change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Plain (`l4.3_plain_selenium.py`) | **3** `By.ID, "daily"` uses (fill, clear, refill) | Locators are duplicated in the test steps |
| POM (`l4.3_page_object_selenium.py`) | **1** (`DutyValidatorPage.DAILY`) | Tests call `page.validate(...)`; locators live on the page object |

The POM also has one `validate()` path for both cases, so weekly/rest/submit/result locators are each declared once instead of being repeated in every test. The POM is the lower-maintenance design for locator churn.
